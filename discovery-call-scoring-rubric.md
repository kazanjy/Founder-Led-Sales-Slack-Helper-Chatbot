# Discovery Call Scoring Rubric

*Generated from `src/lib/call-review/rubric.ts` on September 6, 2026. Re-run `npx tsx scripts/export-discovery-rubric.ts` after changing the rubric.*

**35 items across 4 sections. 70 points total.** Every item is scored 0, 1 or 2 — missed, partial, nailed — and the overall score is their plain sum. There is no weighting beyond the number of items a section contains, so a section's weight IS its item count.

## Section weights

| Section | Items | Max points | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A) Pre-call + Opening | 7 | 14 | 20% |
| B) Discovery Execution Quality | 14 | 28 | 40% |
| C) Relevance Pivot | 6 | 12 | 17% |
| D) Close + Next Step | 8 | 16 | 23% |
| **Total** | **35** | **70** | **100%** |

## A) Pre-call + Opening

*Max 14 points across 7 items.*

### References specific company/person research (not generic)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No research evident, generic opener |
| **1** | Mentions company name but nothing specific |
| **2** | References specific recent event, role detail, or company initiative |

### Rapport: targeted, shows planning, not generic small talk

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No rapport building, jumps straight to business |
| **1** | Generic small talk (weather, weekend, 'how are you') |
| **2** | Targeted rapport that shows planning — references something personal, specific, or clever about the prospect |

### Brings lightness: humor, warmth, or personality early in the call

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No humor or warmth, purely transactional tone throughout |
| **1** | Some attempt at lightness but feels forced or one-note |
| **2** | Genuine humor, a playful comment, or warm personality that makes the conversation feel relaxed and human |

### Time check: confirms end time

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No mention of time |
| **1** | Vague time reference |
| **2** | Explicit time confirmation ('are we good til :30?') |

### Agenda set: states what the call will cover and gets buy-in

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No agenda, dives right in |
| **1** | Mentions what they'll cover but doesn't confirm with prospect |
| **2** | Clear agenda stated and confirmed ('does that work for you?' / 'anything you'd add?') |

### Elevator pitch: concise, compelling 'what we do' (under 60 seconds)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No positioning statement, or rambling multi-minute pitch |
| **1** | Explains what they do but too long or too vague |
| **2** | Crisp, compelling positioning in under 60 seconds that connects to the prospect's world |

### Smooth transition from opening to discovery questions

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Abrupt shift or no clear transition |
| **1** | Awkward transition |
| **2** | Natural bridge from pitch/agenda into first discovery question |

## B) Discovery Execution Quality

*Max 28 points across 14 items.*

### Starts with current state ('how do you do X today?')

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Never asks about current state |
| **1** | Briefly touches on current state |
| **2** | Opens discovery with clear current-state questions |

### Pulls the thread: asks 2nd/3rd-level follow-ups

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | One-and-done questions throughout |
| **1** | Occasionally follows up but mostly surface-level |
| **2** | Consistently digs deeper with follow-up questions |

### Gets at least one concrete recent instance/story

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No specific examples obtained |
| **1** | Gets a vague example |
| **2** | Gets a concrete, recent, specific story or instance |

### Pain is explicit: stated in prospect's words

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Pain is assumed or never surfaced |
| **1** | Rep states the pain, prospect agrees |
| **2** | Prospect articulates the pain in their own words |

### Pain is sized: frequency/volume (per day/week/month)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No quantification attempted |
| **1** | Vague sizing ('a lot', 'frequently') |
| **2** | Specific frequency/volume numbers obtained |

### Impact is sized: time/$/risk/headcount or KPI impact

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No impact discussion |
| **1** | Qualitative impact ('it's costly') |
| **2** | Quantified impact with numbers or ranges |

### Why now: trigger event / urgency / cost of inaction

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No urgency discussion |
| **1** | Vague sense of timing |
| **2** | Clear trigger event or cost of inaction identified |

### Priority check: where this sits vs other initiatives

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not discussed |
| **1** | Briefly mentioned |
| **2** | Clearly established relative priority |

### Current alternatives: tools/workarounds/vendors + why insufficient

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not explored |
| **1** | Knows they use something but not why it's insufficient |
| **2** | Full picture of current solutions and their shortcomings |

### Success definition: what 'better' looks like + measurable outcomes

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not discussed |
| **1** | Vague vision of better |
| **2** | Clear, measurable definition of success |

### Stakeholders: who uses / who signs / who blocks

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not discussed |
| **1** | Knows the signer but not the full picture |
| **2** | Mapped users, decision-maker, and potential blockers |

### Process: next steps in their buying process

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not discussed |
| **1** | Vague understanding |
| **2** | Clear picture of security, legal, procurement steps |

### Disqualifies when appropriate (doesn't force-fit)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Force-fits solution despite poor fit signals |
| **1** | Acknowledges misfit but pushes forward |
| **2** | Appropriately qualifies/disqualifies based on fit |

### Conversation feel: 'beers test' (peer-to-peer, not interrogation)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Feels like an interrogation or scripted pitch |
| **1** | Professional but stiff |
| **2** | Natural, peer-to-peer conversation flow |

| Score | Means |

| **0** | No check-ins, monologue |

### Trial closes: validates resonance

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |

| **2** | Regular trial closes throughout ('is this the kind of thing you meant?') |

## D) Close + Next Step

*Max 16 points across 8 items.*

### Reserves time to close (doesn't run out the clock)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Runs out of time, rushed ending |
| **1** | Leaves some time but feels hurried |
| **2** | Clearly reserves 5+ minutes for close |

### Clear mutual decision: advance or part ways as friends

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Ambiguous ending |
| **1** | Rep pushes for next step without mutual agreement |
| **2** | Clear mutual decision to advance or not |

### Avoids weak close ('let me know what you think' = fail)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | 'Let me know what you think' or similar |
| **1** | Suggests next step but doesn't lock it |
| **2** | Strong, specific close with no ambiguity |

### Next step is concrete: meeting booked with date/time

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No next step or vague 'I'll follow up' |
| **1** | Agrees to meet again but no date set |
| **2** | Meeting booked on calendar before hanging up |

### Next step has purpose: what will be decided/produced

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No agenda for next meeting |
| **1** | Vague purpose |
| **2** | Clear purpose and expected outcome for next call |

### Homework assigned: both sides (data, attendees, docs)

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No homework for either side |
| **1** | One-sided homework only |
| **2** | Both sides have clear action items |

### Confirms stakeholders for next step

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | Not discussed |
| **1** | Vague 'bring your team' |
| **2** | Specific people identified for next meeting |

### Recap: verbal recap + commits to follow-up email

| Score | Means |
| :---: | --- |
| **0** | No recap, no follow-up commitment |
| **1** | Brief recap or follow-up mention |
| **2** | Clear verbal recap and explicit follow-up email commitment |

## Red flags

Noted separately from the score. These are call-level failures rather than item-level misses, so a call can score respectably and still carry one.

| Red flag | How it's detected |
| --- | --- |
| Demo-first with no discovery | Rep shows product/deck before asking discovery questions |
| Prospect talks <40% of the time | Estimated prospect talk time is below 40% |
| No quantified impact attempt | Rep never attempts to size the pain or impact with numbers |
| No buying process discussion | No mention of procurement, legal, security, or decision process |
| Ends without a scheduled next step | Call ends without a concrete meeting booked on calendar |

## How the score is computed

`calculateOverallScore()` sums every item score and every section max. Two consequences worth knowing:

- **An unscored item counts as zero, not as excluded.** The denominator is the full 70 regardless of how many items the reviewer actually returned, so a partial review reads as a low score rather than a short one.
- **Red flags do not subtract.** They are reported alongside the score, not deducted from it.
